4 Dropdown & Template Customization Guidelines Safe Expansion Without Broken Formulas

Tailoring the Tracker to Your Agency

Every community health service and ACCO has unique job titles, local funding streams, and clinical supervision structures. You can customize every dropdown safely by following these three golden rules.

Golden Rule #1

The Middle-Row Insertion Rule

When adding a new staff member or client, never type beneath the bottom row of the table. Instead, right-click any row in the middle and choose "Insert 1 row above".

Why it works: Google Sheets automatically stretches all formula ranges (e.g. A4:A28 becomes A4:A29) without stranding your new data outside the KPI counters.
Golden Rule #2

Editing Dropdown Lists

To add new funding bodies (e.g., "Victorian Family Violence Reform") or custom sector roles, select the column, click Data > Data validation, and add items to the chip list.

Safe Practice: Keep existing values untouched and simply append new items to the bottom of the validation list so historic records don't trigger invalid warnings.
Golden Rule #3

Formula Shield Drag-Down

When you insert a new row, the grey cell may start blank. Click the grey cell directly above it and press Ctrl + D (or drag the small blue corner handle down).

Instant Shield: This copies the exact nested formula with relative row references, armoring your new row in one second.
