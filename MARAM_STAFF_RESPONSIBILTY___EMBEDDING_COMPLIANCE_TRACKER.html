The Two Recovery Lines of Defence

Keep these two recovery commands memorised for all administrative staff:

  1. 1
    Defence 1: Immediate Undo (The 10-Second Reflex)
    Press Ctrl + Z (or Cmd + Z on Mac) immediately. This rolls back the cell alteration instantly.
  2. 2
    Defence 2: Version History Restore (The Deep Safety Net)
    Navigate to File > Version history > See version history (shortcut: Ctrl + Alt + Shift + H).
  3. 3
    Defence 3: Snapshot Naming
    Select the timestamp prior to the mistake and click "Restore this version". Best practice: Name your version before quarterly audits (e.g. "Pre-Audit Q3 Baseline").
  1. 1

Visual Cell Colour System Key

The tracker utilizes a disciplined, high-contrast visual taxonomy. Every team member entering staff details must understand the clear operational division between Manual Entry and Formula Engines.
SAFE TO TYPE
[ WHITE CELLS ]
Unlocked Data Entry Fields. Staff names, completion dates, accreditation numbers, and reflective practice qualitative notes.
LOCKED FORMULA
[ GREY CELLS ]
Protected Calculation Engines. Automated formulas that look up MARAM Tiers, calculate compliance percentages, and trigger statutory alerts. Never type here.
WARNING
⚠️ DUE SOON (Soft Gold)
Screening / Training Expiry Alert. Automatically triggered when a WWCC, Police Check, NDIS Check, or MARAM accreditation is within 60 days of expiration.
CRITICAL
🟥 EXPIRED (Soft Red)
Non-Compliant / Expired Check. The check date has passed or mandatory MARAM victim-survivor training has not been undertaken. Immediate triage required.

MARAM Tier 1–4 Definition Guide & Responsibility Matrix

The Victorian Family Violence Protection Act 2008 requires prescribed organisations to align their workforce with the Multi-Agency Risk Assessment and Management (MARAM) Framework across statutory responsibilities 1 through 10.
TIER 1 • FOUNDATION

Identification Tier

Statutory Responsibilities: 1–2, 5–6, 9–10
Focus: Recognizing observable signs of family violence, active listening, sensitive inquiry, trauma-informed boundary management, and safe, warm referral pathways.
Target Staff Roles:
  • Receptionists & Front Desk Staff
  • Administrative Coordinators
  • Community Facilities & Transport Drivers
  • General Intake & Call Handlers
TIER 2 • INTERMEDIATE

Intermediate Tier

Statutory Responsibilities: 1–4, 5–8, 9–10
Focus: Structured intermediate risk assessment, perpetrator pattern recognition, dynamic safety planning, and collaborative multi-agency case management under FVISS/CISS.
Target Staff Roles:
  • Intensive & Generalist Case Managers
  • Housing & Tenancy Officers
  • Youth & Family Support Workers
  • Community Health Nurses & AOD Clinicians
TIER 3 • SPECIALIST

Comprehensive Tier

Statutory Responsibilities: 1 through 10 (Full Depth)
Focus: Advanced risk assessment, complex forensic indicators, assessing coercive control, high-risk safety plans, children's assessments, and leading multi-agency risk panels (RAMP).
Target Staff Roles:
  • Specialist Family Violence Advocates
  • Crisis Refuge Practitioners
  • Men's Behaviour Change Facilitators
  • High-Risk Triage Specialists
TIER 4 • GOVERNANCE

Leadership & Supervision

Oversight, Reflective Practice & Audit
Focus: System-level policy alignment, qualitative reflective supervision, continuous clinical auditing, statutory reporting, and multi-agency executive governance.
Target Staff Roles:
  • Practice Leads & Clinical Supervisors
  • Program Managers & Operations Leads
  • Executive Directors & Board Compliance Chairs
  • Quality, Risk & Accreditation Managers

Step-by-Step Setup Workflow & Dropdown Engine

The core engine of the LEW CREATIONS tracker relies on a master relational link: your definitions in 1. Role Matrix Setup dynamically feed the dropdown menus in MASTER_STAFF_RT. Follow the 4-phase sequence:
PHASE 01

1. Define Roles in Matrix

Open the 1. Role Matrix Setup tab. Add every distinct job title across your organisation in Column A. Set their assigned MARAM Tier and required checks.

PHASE 02

2. Set Validation Dropdown

In MASTER_STAFF_RT, Column C references the Role Matrix via Data Validation: ='1. Role Matrix Setup'!$A$2:$A$50. This prevents spelling discrepancies.

PHASE 03

3. Input Staff & Dates

Only type into White Cells. Select the role from the dropdown. The grey formula columns instantly pull the mandatory MARAM Tier and screening rules.

PHASE 04

4. Verify Executive Dashboard

Check 4. Executive Compliance Dashboard. Confirm the compliance percentage matches your HR records before submitting audit statements.
