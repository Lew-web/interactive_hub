Multi-Grant Apportionment Rules & Interactive Simulator

INTERACTIVE TOOL

In community services and ACCO environments, programs frequently share operational resources—such as office rent, regional outreach vehicles, internet lines, and shared casework coordinators. Billing 100% of these expenses to a single grant breaches statutory service agreements.

The 100% Split Rule & Verification Formula

In 2. Expense Ledger, columns E through J capture up to 3 grants with percentage splits. Column K runs the verification logic: =IF(ROUND(SUM(Split1, Split2, Split3), 2)=1.0, "✅ Perfect", "⚠️ Allocation Error"). Only expenses marked ✅ Perfect will flow into the acquittal statement!

Automated Burn-Rate Health Alerts & Pacing Intelligence

PACING GOVERNANCE

Funding bodies audit financial milestones rigorously. If a community organisation spends down funds too slowly, government bodies claw back unspent balances upon contract expiry. If funds are burned too rapidly, the agency faces an unbudgeted cash shortfall.

🟩 BUDGET SAFE

Healthy Pacing

Financial burn rate tracks within ±10% of elapsed contract time. Operational activities and staffing are aligned with the approved schedule.

🟨 VARIANCE WATCH

Burn Acceleration / Idle Funds

Expenditure is running > 15% ahead of timeline, or less than 60 days remain before acquittal due date with substantial uncommitted funds.

🟥 OVERSPEND ALERT

Critical Overrun

Expenditure has exceeded 95% of approved budget pool prior to final project completion, or cumulative expenses exceed total funding.
