3 Standard Operating Procedures

The 5-Step Fleet Operational Lifecycle

From asset registration to trip logging and formal grant acquittal audit locks, this standard operating flow ensures audit-readiness under ATO commercial logbook mandates.

01

Fleet Asset Registration

TAB: FLEET_ROSTER (Cols A–E, G)

Register every vehicle with its unique Vehicle ID (e.g. VEH-001), Registration Plate (e.g. 1AA-1BB), Make/Model, Branch Depot, and assigned Fuel Card ID. Enter baseline odometer into Last Serviced to kick off service interval tracking.

02

Carpool Trip Logging (Driver Check-In)

TAB: MASTER_CARPOOL_LOG (Cols A–J)

Drivers log each operational journey upon vehicle return: Date, Driver Name, Rego, End Odometer, Funding Stream, Trip Purpose, Tolls, and Fuel Card expenses.

03

Automated Start Odometer Telemetry Sync

AUTOMATED: MASTER_CARPOOL_LOG (Cols D & F) & FLEET_DASHBOARD!E5

When Dashboard control toggle FLEET_DASHBOARD!E5 is TRUE, the Start Odometer automatically synchronizes from that specific vehicle’s previous End Odometer. Total Trip KMs immediately calculate in formula shield Col F.

04

Service & Lease Turnover Vigilance

AUTOMATED: FLEET_ROSTER (Cols H–J) & FLEET_DASHBOARD

The formula engine tracks vehicle mileage against standard 10,000 km maintenance intervals. Automatically triggers SERVICE DUE SOON (<1,000 km window), SERVICING OVERDUE, and flags vehicles over 100,000 km as ELIGIBLE FOR RESALE.

05

Compliance Reconciliation & Row-Level Audit Lock

TAB: MASTER_CARPOOL_LOG (Cols K–L) & FLEET_DASHBOARD!E4

Compliance officers verify fuel receipts and journey purpose against driver logbook flags (Col K). Once approved, toggling the Lock Flag (Col L) or global Audit Lock (E4) permanently protects the entry from unauthorized tampering.

The 30-Second Safe Dropdown Guide
