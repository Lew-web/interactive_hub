Operational Architecture

Dual-Terminology Governance & Care Leaver Context

Victorian CYFA 2005 Alignment

The Youth Program & Care Leaver Transition Hub provides a rigorous operational database to manage the critical transition cliff-edge faced by young people (ages 15–21) transitioning from Victorian Out-of-Home Care (OOHC). Built specifically for mainstream youth providers and Aboriginal Community Controlled Organisations (ACCOs), it unites statutory post-care milestones under Better Futures and Home Stretch with flexible youth brokerage ledgers, frontline qualitative yarning logs, and MARAM risk triage.

Mainstream Victorian Compliance Track

  • Children, Youth and Families Act 2005 (CYFA): Mandates post-care transition planning starting at age 15 and supports voluntary extended care up to age 21.
  • Better Futures & Home Stretch Guidelines: Tracks housing allowances, independent living life-skills goals, and flexible brokerage acquittals ($10k allocation pools).
  • MARAM Risk Framework: Systematically logs youth family violence triage, screening tiers, and safety plan completions.

ACCO Cultural Governance & Priority Reform 2

  • Closing the Gap Priority Reform 2: Empowers community-controlled service delivery, self-determination, and cultural authority in transition planning.
  • Yarning Circles & Qualitative Evidence: Frontline logs recognize Elder guidance, Kinship reconnection, and Connection to Country as protective audit evidence.
  • Dual-Terminology Practice: Pairs quantitative contract KPIs with culturally safe youth narratives and mentoring consultations.

Data Sovereignty & Zero-Access Guarantee

This system operates 100% locally inside your organisation's private Google Drive (via copy model). Neither LEW CREATIONS nor any third party ever sees or stores de-identified youth records, MARAM disclosures, Elder yarning notes, or financial brokerage receipts. Fully aligned with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Victorian Health Records Act 2001.

Cell Safety Rules: White Input vs. Grey Formula Shields

Unbreakable Cell Protocol

To maintain financial audit readiness and eliminate formula corruption across multi-practitioner teams, LEW CREATIONS templates employ a strict two-tone cell sovereignty discipline. Caseworkers and team leaders must follow one golden rule: Only type in pure white cells. Grey cells are locked shields.

WHITE CELLS (#FFFFFF)

EDITABLE ZONE

Frontline Data Entry Territory: Type de-identified youth information, operational updates, and qualitative notes here.

  • Qualitative Yarning Summaries & Goals Met

  • Running Brokerage Remaining Balances
